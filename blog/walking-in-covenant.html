Walking in covenant: God keeps His promises

By Bishop Henry Muzhari 25 September 2026 1 min read

Throughout Scripture, God reveals Himself as a covenant-keeping God. When He made a promise to Abraham, He bound Himself by His own name, because there was no one greater to swear by (Hebrews 6:13).

Covenant is relationship

A contract says, “I will do my part if you do yours.” A covenant says, “I am yours, and you are mine.” That is the heart of the Gospel: in Christ, God has drawn us into His family.

“Know therefore that the Lord your God, He is God, the faithful God who keeps covenant and mercy for a thousand generations with those who love Him.” — Deuteronomy 7:9

Living it out

This week, take a moment each morning to thank God for one promise He has kept in your life. Share it with someone in your family or small group. Faith grows when testimonies are spoken.
