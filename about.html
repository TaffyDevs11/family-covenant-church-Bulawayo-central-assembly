About us

A Spirit-filled family in the heart of Bulawayo, raising disciples who love God, love people and transform their world.

Who we are

A family bound by covenant.

Family Covenant Church Bulawayo Central Assembly is part of the Family Covenant Church family. We believe the local church is God's family on earth — a place where people of every age and background are welcomed, discipled and released into their purpose.

01

Our vision

To be a covenant family that reveals the love of Christ, builds strong homes and raises a generation that carries God's presence into every sphere of life.

02

Our mission

To worship God in spirit and truth, teach His Word with clarity, care for one another as family and reach our city with the Gospel.

General OverseersFCC

Our Senior Pastors

Bishop Henry and Reverend Eunice Muzhari serve as the General Overseers of Family Covenant Church and the Senior Pastors of Bulawayo Central Assembly. Together they lead with a father's and mother's heart — teaching the Word, building families and raising leaders.
